LearnableMeta is designed to teach clues in context instead of asking you to memorize long lists. A supported map associates each location with a short explanation of the clue worth noticing there.

## Play a map

<Steps>

1. Confirm that the [LearnableMeta userscript is installed](/getting-started/#install-learnablemeta).
2. Open a fresh browser tab or restart your browser.
3. Choose a map from [learnablemeta.com/maps](https://learnablemeta.com/maps).
4. Open the map in GeoGuessr and play the round normally.
5. After making your guess, read the LearnableMeta note on the results screen.
6. Compare the note with the panorama before moving to the next round.

</Steps>

The maps are especially useful in **No Move** games: recognizing the Google car, poles, road lines, bollards, signs, and landscape without searching around is an important part of the skill being trained.

<Aside type="tip">
  Pause after each guess and locate the documented clue in the panorama. Connecting the explanation to what you just saw is more memorable than reading the note alone.
</Aside>

## Where hints appear

LearnableMeta displays its note after you submit a guess. It does not reveal the answer while you are still choosing a location.

If no note appears, first confirm that you opened GeoGuessr in a new tab after installing the script. Then work through the [troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting/).
