LearnableMeta teaches useful GeoGuessr clues while you play. Its maps contain tagged locations, and its browser userscript displays an explanation after each guess.

## What you need

- A GeoGuessr account
- A supported browser
- A userscript manager such as Tampermonkey
- The LearnableMeta userscript

## Start learning

<Steps>

1. **Install a userscript manager and LearnableMeta.**

   Follow the instructions for your browser or device.

2. **Open a fresh browser tab.**

   Tabs that were already open during installation may not load the userscript. Restarting the browser is the safest option.

3. **Choose a LearnableMeta map.**

   Browse the [available maps](https://learnablemeta.com/maps) and open one in GeoGuessr.

4. **Play and review each clue.**

   Make your guess normally. On the results screen, LearnableMeta shows the clue attached to that location.

</Steps>
